# Robot fighter asset

- **Mesh and textures:** [Robot character](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/robot-character-2fe59c8ef49547d2a1e0a65506701a7e) by assetfactory, Sketchfab Free Standard license. Used and recolored in this game; the original source files are not redistributed.
- **Rig and animations:** [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com) (Adobe). Clips used: Punching, Punch Combo, Elbow Punching, Kicking, Mma Kick, Flip Kick, Chapa-Giratoria, Center Block, Body Block, Left Block, Right Block, Head Hit, Hit On Side Of Head, Hit To Body, Big Stomach Hit, Hit On Legs, Stunned, Knocked Out, Getting Up, Fighting Idle, Walking, Walking Backwards, Jog Strafe Left, Jog Strafe Right, Injured Stumble Idle, Stumble Backwards.

# Ghoul fighter asset

- **Mesh, textures and one animation:** Mixamo character "Parasite" and the Mixamo animation that came with it, from [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com) (Adobe), used under the Mixamo terms (called Ghoul in the game). Baked onto the Robot fighter's Mixamo skeleton by bone name with its own skin weights; textures resized to 1024 px for the web (specular maps dropped). Its leap-and-crouch animation is retargeted onto the same skeleton (kept in place) and used only as Ghoul's Victory / Taunt; every other move plays the shared Mixamo clips listed above. The original FBX is not redistributed.
