# Sound licenses

Robot Fight uses only public-domain (CC0) and royalty-free clips that do not require credit. Sources are listed here for the record. Files in `sounds/` are short, recompressed excerpts used in the game; original pack archives are not redistributed.

## Kenney — CC0 1.0

No attribution required. Optional credit is appreciated: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl).

- **Impact Sounds** — https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds  
  Metal, punch, plate, tin, soft-body, mining, and bell impacts; carpet footsteps (used as canvas/mat steps).  
  Game files: `hit_metal_*`, `hit_punch_*`, `block_*`, `cage_*`, `bounce_*`, `step_*`, `fall_*`, `tear_mine_*`, `bell_*`.

- **Sci-fi Sounds** — https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds  
  Also on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds).  
  Engine loops trimmed to short servo bursts; force-field one-shots; explosion crunches for parts tearing off.  
  Game files: `servo_*`, `field_*`, `tear_000.ogg`, `tear_002.ogg`, `tear_004.ogg`.

- **Interface Sounds** — https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds  
  Also on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds).  
  UI clicks, ticks, confirm, and close.  
  Game files: `ui_click.ogg`, `ui_click_2.ogg`, `ui_tick.ogg`, `ui_confirm.ogg`, `ui_close.ogg`.

License text: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
